were kept: their memory, scale and correctness results do not depend on other
processes' CPU use (their incidental timings, such as the cancellation
latencies, do, and are not verdict inputs).

Replaced by: the same four groups run again into the parent directory with
run_all.sh --quiet-below 0.3 (load.sh): before every process the script waits
until the other processes use fewer than 0.3 CPUs over 2 seconds, and it
measures a process again when they used 0.3 CPUs or more on average while it
ran; load.csv in the parent directory records the load during every process.

These rows are kept for the record, outside summarize.py's reach (it reads
only the directories it is given). timing.csv here holds these runs'
